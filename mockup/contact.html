FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Membership & dues

How do I become a member?

Register from the Sign In page — a committee member follows up to complete your family record within a few days.

How do I pay family dues?

Sign in and open Payments & Dues — cards, bank transfer, and Zelle are all accepted.

Donations & receipts

How do I get a donation receipt?

A receipt is emailed automatically the moment a donation is processed — no request needed.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes — JCA is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, Tax ID 13-3135532.

Visiting

Do I need to be Jain to visit?

No — anyone is welcome to visit, take darshan, and ask questions, any day during open hours.

Can I take photos?

Photography is welcome between rituals. During aarti itself, please keep phones silent and put away.

Facility booking

Can I book the Dining Hall for a private event?

Yes, subject to the Facility Use Policy — call (718) 478-9141 to check availability.
