Explore the five traditions

The Dadawadi Temple of Shri Jin Kushal Suri Ji Maharaj, the gemstone Asthapad Maha Tirth under open sky, a roof garden, and the spacious Dining Hall and kitchen that seat over 150.

Learn more →

The Digambar Shri Adinathji Temple, the Shrimad Rajchandra Meditation Hall, and the Library — home to weekly Pathshala classes, the JCA office and a scholars' room.

Adinathji Temple →Shrimad Hall →

Two traditions on one floor: the Shri Mahavir Swami Temple and the Upashray Lecture Hall, which seats about 200 and opens onto the temple.

Mahavir Swami Temple →Upashray Lecture Hall →

The welcome level: entry vestibules, reception, office and the senior center. Below it, the cellar holds the multipurpose hall, youth center, mini theatre, children's room and a rotating art gallery.
