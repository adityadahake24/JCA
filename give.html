Sponsorship for Bhojanshala

A Day of Meals, in Someone’s Name

Jain Center of America requests you to participate in Sponsoring Bhojanshala at least once a year.

Sponsoring Bhojanshala for a day is only $251. Your Sponsorship will help us to conduct Bhojanshala activities at JCA. Some of the occasions that you can sponsor are Birthdays, Wedding Anniversaries, Punya Tithis for any relative or any other occasion that you would like to share with our Jain Community.

Jain Center of America is a non-profit organization and all your Donations to Sponsor Bhojanshala are Tax Deductible.

The Name of the Sponsor will be displayed on the main Notice Board of the Jain Center as well as the JCA Website for the entire week. All the sponsors for the week will be jointly celebrated on weekends.

Our goal is to get Sponsorship dates for the entire year (365 days) with our members support.
