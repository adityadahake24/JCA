Six Ways to Open the Door

Landing-page hero options for Iteration 1. Open any card to see the full page with the real menu, then use the bar at the bottom to flip between options. Try the theme toggle and a phone-width window.

The one on the live home page

Current hero

Building photo behind the title, with upcoming-events and Panchang glass cards. Kept as the fallback.

Open full page
Lit arches = the building’s floors

A · Five Doorways

Five arches rise like the temple’s four floors and glow in turn like lamps. Each links to a shrine; quick pills reach the other sections.

Open full page
Mandala site map

B · Living Orbit

The five site sections orbit a turning mandala around a live Aarti chip. The whole site is visible in the first screen.

Open full page
Sky follows the time of day

C · Dawn to Dusk

Sun or moon travels an arc over a temple skyline, with a rotating word (worship, learn, celebrate, serve, give) and quick-link pills.

Open full page
Line-drawn building as menu

D · Five-Floor Elevation

A drawn elevation of the five-storey building. Floors light in sequence and link to each tradition.

Open full page
New — real sun, draggable

6 · Sun Dial

The sun sits where it is on your clock. Drag it along the sunrise-to-sunset line and the sky, the temple’s light and its shadow follow. The real temple, relit live.
