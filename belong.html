Volunteer Sign Up

Lend Your Hands

We appreciate your interest in Volunteering at Jain Center of America, NY. Volunteerism fosters a sense of community and belonging. It brings unexpected joy and the pleasure of giving back. Make new friends and reconnect with old ones.

As Gandhiji said “Be the change that you wish to see in the world.”

JCA is living proof of Jain Unity celebrating our Diversity. Jain Center of America is managed by volunteers and there are many ways and activities that you can volunteer for.

Volunteer with JCA
Bhojanshala kitchen shifts, Pathshala teaching, festival setup, and more — sign up for what fits your week.
