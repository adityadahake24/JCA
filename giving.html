Ways to give

Choose what works for you

Every method reaches the same causes above. A receipt is emailed automatically for tax purposes.

Credit or debit card
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover — processed instantly online.
Bank transfer
Direct transfer from your bank — ask reception or your bank for JCA's routing details.
Zelle
Send directly to donations@nyjaincenter.org — no fees, funds arrive the same day.
Matching donation
Many employers match charitable gifts dollar-for-dollar — ask your HR department, then let us know.
Cheque by mail

Accountability

Where your gift goes

JCA publishes an annual financial statement and reports to the membership at the General Body Meeting each December. Every restricted gift — Bhojanshala, Pathshala Scholarship, or a specific festival sponsorship — is spent only on that cause.
